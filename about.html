My Story

I earned a BS in Informatics from the University of Massachusetts Amherst in 2023, with a minor in Natural Resource Economics. Before and during college, I worked in municipal roles, from data entry in Excel and SQL-based systems to IT network support.

From 2023 to 2025 I worked in special education for the City of Chicopee, designing data-driven interventions, tracking measurable student goals, and advising educators on classroom accommodations for students with autism. In 2026 I joined Viability as a Technology Integration Specialist 2, where I lead the (VR)2 initiative, build interactive dashboards, and manage the platforms and devices the team relies on.
