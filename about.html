About Me

An informaticist applying a data science background to technology integration and special education.

My Story

I earned a BS in Informatics from the University of Massachusetts Amherst in 2023, with a minor in Natural Resource Economics. Before and during college I worked in municipal roles, from data entry in Excel and SQL-based systems to IT network support.

From 2023 to 2025 I worked in special education with the City of Chicopee, designing data-driven interventions, tracking measurable student goals, and advising educators on classroom accommodations for students with autism. In 2026 I joined Viability as a Technology Integration Specialist 2, where I lead the (VR)2 initiative, build interactive dashboards, and manage the platforms and devices the team relies on.

What I Bring

Three strengths I rely on in every role.

Analytical Approach

I use data to set measurable goals, shape interventions, and track real-world outcomes.

Technical Toolkit

I work in RStudio, STATA, SQL, Python, and pandas, build interactive dashboards, and administer ArborXR, Jamf, and Apple Business Manager.

Collaboration

I communicate clearly, work well with teachers, colleagues, and clients, and stay organized across competing priorities.

My Journey

Key steps from municipal data work to informatics, special education, and technology integration.

  1. 2015

    Data Entry Associate

  2. 2019

    IT Network Technician

  3. 2023

    Graduation and Special Education

    Graduated from UMass Amherst and began designing data-driven accommodations as a Special Education Interventionist.

  4. 2024

    Autism Specialist

    Designed data-driven behavioral interventions, monitored measurable student goals, and advised educators on accommodations.

  5. Today

    Technology Integration Specialist 2

    At Viability, I lead the (VR)2 initiative, build interactive dashboards and Excel analyses, and manage ArborXR, Jamf, and Apple Business Manager.
